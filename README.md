# StudyMate – Student Study Planner

## About the Project

StudyMate is a mobile study planning app designed to help college students organize their study activities, assignments, schedules, and academic progress in one place.

## Problem Statement

College students often face difficulties managing assignments, study schedules, deadlines, and progress across multiple subjects. StudyMate aims to provide a simple and organized solution for these problems.

## Target Users

- College students aged 18–24
- Students managing multiple subjects and assignments
- Students who want to maintain a consistent study routine

## Key Features

- Daily study task management
- Study schedule planning
- Assignment deadline tracking
- Task priority management
- Subject-wise progress tracking
- Weekly progress overview

## User Research

The research focused on understanding common problems faced by college students, including:

- Forgetting assignment deadlines
- Difficulty planning study sessions
- Difficulty prioritizing tasks
- Difficulty tracking academic progress
- Balancing college work and personal study time

## User Persona

**Name:** Ananya  
**Age:** 20  
**Occupation:** 2nd-Year College Student

Ananya wants to become more organized with her studies, complete assignments on time, prepare systematically for exams, and maintain a consistent study routine.

## Design Thinking Process

### 1. Empathize
Identified common challenges faced by college students.

### 2. Define
Defined the problem of managing daily study activities and deadlines.

### 3. Ideate
Proposed StudyMate as a simple study planning solution.

### 4. Prototype
Created low-fidelity mobile wireframes using Figma.

### 5. Test
The wireframes can be reviewed with students to identify usability issues and improve the design.
